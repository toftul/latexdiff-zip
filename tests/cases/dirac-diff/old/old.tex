\section*{Old title}

Diract equation
\begin{equation}
    (i\hbar \gamma^\mu \partial_\mu - mc)\,\psi = 0,
\end{equation}
where $\gamma^\mu$ are the gamma matrices, $\psi$ is the four-component Dirac spinor, and $\partial_\mu = \partial/\partial x^\mu$.
